Keys in thirty days

In the first week, we talk before we send anything. Budget, timeline, whether you need the visa, whether the home is for living in or letting. Then a shortlist of homes we have walked through ourselves.

Every home on it arrives with its service charge per square foot, its last transfer price from DLD records and any snagging history.

By the second week, you have seen them all in one day, driven in the order that avoids Sheikh Zayed Road at rush hour. We negotiate, sign Form F, the Land Department’s sale agreement, and hold the 10% deposit as trustee.

Buying from overseas? Viewings run live on video, and a power of attorney lets us sign for you.

On transfer day, the developer has issued its no-objection certificate, your bank has released the mortgage, and everyone meets at a DLD trustee office. The title deed is printed in your name before you leave the building.

We stay for the handover: DEWA connection, chiller account, access cards and the Ejari if you are letting.

Can I buy if I don’t live in the UAE?

Yes. Foreign nationals can buy freehold in Dubai’s designated areas, which include the Palm, the Marina, Downtown, Dubai Hills and Emirates Hills, with a passport alone. You do not need residency to own.

Does buying give me a visa?

A home worth AED 750,000 or more qualifies for a two-year renewable property investor visa. At AED 2 million or more you can apply for the ten-year Golden Visa, including on a mortgaged or off-plan home.

What do I pay on top of the price?

The 4% Land Department fee, about AED 4,200 for the trustee office, a few hundred dirhams of admin and 2% brokerage plus VAT. Mortgages add registration and bank fees. The statement above adds them all for your price.

Can I get a mortgage from overseas?

Yes. UAE banks lend to non-residents, usually up to half the value. Residents buying a first home can borrow up to 80% under AED 5 million and 70% above it.

Off-plan or ready?

Off-plan costs less to enter and is paid in stages, often 60/40 or with payments after handover, but you wait two to four years. A ready home earns rent from the first month and you can inspect exactly what you buy.

What will it cost to keep each year?

Service charges are set per square foot and published in the RERA index; on the communities above they run from about AED 12 to 35. There is no annual property tax. We put the real figure on every listing.
